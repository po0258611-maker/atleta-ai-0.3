## 1. Brand Essence & Vision

**Athleta AI** represents the pinnacle of evidence-based biomechanical workout prescription, real-time recovery synthesis, and metabolic nutrition engineering. 

### Brand Values:
1. **Scientific Rigor**: Grounded in tension mechanical load, autoregulation, and hypertrophy research.
2. **Biomechanical Precision**: 3D ray-traced anatomical mapping and high-definition targeted muscle engagement.
3. **Sleek Athletic Luxury**: Obsidian dark canvas (`#09090B`), volcanic graphite, and electric crimson (`#E11D48`).
4. **Uncompromising Clarity**: Technical jargon is replaced with high-end, empowering, and memorable terminology.

## 5. Tone of Voice & Copywriting Guidelines

2. **Eliminate Fluff**: Avoid generic marketing buzzwords ("supercharge", "magic formula").
3. **Bilingual Elegance**: Portuguese primary with elevated English performance sub-titling for international readiness (*"Command Center", "Fullbody Matrix", "NutriFlux"*).
